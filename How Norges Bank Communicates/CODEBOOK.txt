Codebook

Project

Communication on interest rates from Norges Bank

Corpus boundary

The material was collected on 2026-04-04 from the English-language Norges Bank website.

The public release covers:
40 speeches
40 press releases
80 documents in total

The project treats the paragraph as the unit of analysis. The public paragraph-level file contains one row per coded paragraph retained after scraping and cleaning.

This is a bounded descriptive corpus. It is not a full archive of all Norges Bank communication, and it does not include media coverage or responses from other actors.

Data note

The full text on the website is owned by Norges Bank and is therefore not included in the uploaded dataset.

The public release contains only:
coded variables
publication dates and times
source type
source URLs
paragraph and document identifiers
simple derived metadata

The public release does not redistribute the full paragraph text or full document text from the website.

Coding approach

The project uses manual content analysis. Paragraphs were extracted from the selected webpages, cleaned, and then coded for one primary communicative function and one primary substantive topic.

This follows standard content-analysis practice in which the unit of analysis, category system, and coding dimensions are stated explicitly.

Data files

data/coded_paragraphs.csv
Paragraph-level public data file.

Variables in coded_paragraphs.csv
primary_function_no: Norwegian label for the paragraph's primary communicative function.
primary_function_en: English label for the paragraph's primary communicative function.
primary_topic_no: Norwegian label for the paragraph's primary substantive topic.
primary_topic_en: English label for the paragraph's primary substantive topic.
category: Document type. Values: Press release or Speech.
url: Source URL on Norges Bank's website.
title: Scraped page title.
published_date: Publication date for the source page.
published_time: Publication time where available on the source page.
paragraph_type: Scraped HTML node type, usually p, li, or blockquote.
paragraph_id: Paragraph number within document after scraping and ordering.
paragraph_nwords: Word count for the cleaned paragraph.
doc_id: Document identifier derived from the URL.
paragraph_uid: Unique paragraph identifier derived from doc_id and paragraph_id.
year: Year derived from published_date.
month: Month derived from published_date.

data/document_inventory.csv
Document-level inventory with one row per speech or press release.

Variables in document_inventory.csv
doc_id: Document identifier derived from the URL.
category: Document type. Values: Press release or Speech.
url: Source URL on Norges Bank's website.
title: Scraped page title.
published_date: Publication date for the source page.
year: Year derived from published_date.
month: Month derived from published_date.
n_paragraphs: Number of cleaned paragraphs retained for the document.

Coding dimensions

Primary communicative function
Economic assessment / diagnosis: Describes or assesses current economic conditions, trends, or indicators.
Policy explanation / justification: Explains, defends, or justifies a policy decision, policy stance, or institutional action.
Outlook / forward guidance: Refers to future expectations, likely developments, or prospective policy orientation.
Risk / uncertainty: Highlights uncertainty, vulnerabilities, or downside and upside risks.
Institutional accountability / mandate: Explains Norges Bank's mandate, role, responsibility, or decision procedure.
Other / administrative / not codable: Administrative text, headings, ancillary information, or non-substantive material.

Primary topic
Inflation and prices: Inflation, prices, cost pressures, or related price developments.
Interest rates and monetary stance: Policy rates, interest-rate setting, monetary stance, or rate-path reasoning.
Economic activity and labor market: Growth, output, demand, employment, wages, or labor market developments.
Exchange rate and external conditions: Exchange rate developments, international conditions, imported conditions, or external shocks.
Financial stability and banking: Banking, credit conditions, financial vulnerabilities, or financial stability issues.
Other / administrative / not codable: Administrative text, headings, ancillary information, or non-substantive material.

Analytic note

The public paragraph-level file retains the coded corpus, including rows coded as Other / administrative / not codable. The figures use a filtered analytic sample that excludes rows with missing values on the plotted variables and rows coded as Other / administrative / not codable.
