RESULTS

Main findings
The analysis examines 47 Facebook posts published by Lars Løkke Rasmussen in the final three weeks before the 2026 Danish election.

Figure 1: number of posts by story type
The most frequently used story type was "Midten som ansvar og balanse" with 17 posts.
This was followed by:
- Posts trying to gather support, labelled "Samling mot splittelse": 13 posts
- Posts containing a personal angle, focusing on Rasmussen himself, which was laballed "Den personlige": 11 posts
- Posts that directly mentiones the election, labelled "Valgkamplogistikk eller annet": 6 posts

Figure 2: overperformance by story type
Overperformance is measured as the difference between a story type's share of total engagement and its share of all posts.
The clearest pattern is that the personal story type generated more engagement than its publication volume would suggest.
- Posts containing a personal angle, focusing on Rasmussen himself, which was laballed "Den personlige": +2.5 percentage points
- Posts that directly mentiones the election, labelled "Valgkamplogistikk eller annet": +1.9 percentage points
- Posts that frames the political center as responsoible and blanaced politic, labelled"Midten som ansvar og balanse": -0.7 percentage points
- Posts trying to gather support, labelled "Samling mot splittelse": -3.7 percentage points

Substantive interpretation
The campaign was published most heavily through a center-oriented and responsibility-focused style, but the more personal posts generated relatively stronger engagement.

This suggests that what Rasmussen published most often was not the same as what generated the strongest response.
