Dataset overview
The original dataset consists of 47 Facebook posts published on Lars Løkke Rasmussen's Facebook page in the final three weeks before the Danish election, from 3 March to 24 March 2026.

Why the dataset is not included
The full dataset is withheld because it contains Facebook-derived posts. To reduce redistribution risk, neither full post text nor a public data extract is included here.

Core dataset elements in the private working file
- post number
- page name
- political party
- publication date
- engagement variables:
  - likes
  - sad
  - care
  - love
  - haha
  - wow
  - angry
  - comments
  - shares
- visual indicator
- coded strategy variables:
  - gpt_strategy
  - hovedstrategi
  - strategy_label

Analytical units
One row equals one Facebook post.

Derived measures used in the analysis
- reactions_total: sum of reaction counts
- engagement_total: reactions_total + comments + shares
- number of posts by story type
- overperformance by story type, defined as the difference between a story type's share of total engagement and share of all posts

Story types used in the analysis
- Midten som ansvar og balanse
- Samling mot splittelse
- Den personlige
- Valgkamplogistikk eller annet
